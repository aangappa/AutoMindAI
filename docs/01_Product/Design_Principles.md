# Purpose

This document defines the engineering and product principles that guide every design decision within AutoMind AI.

Whenever a new feature is proposed, it should align with these principles.

---

# ADP-1 — Customer Before Car

AutoMind must understand the customer before recommending any vehicle.

Recommendations should be based on lifestyle, budget, driving habits, ownership goals, and personal preferences.

---

# ADP-2 — Explain Every Recommendation

Every recommendation must explain:

- Why it was selected.
- Why alternatives ranked lower.
- What trade-offs exist.

Recommendations should never appear as black-box decisions.

---

# ADP-3 — Show Trade-offs

Every vehicle has strengths and weaknesses.

AutoMind must present both.

The goal is informed decision-making rather than promoting a particular vehicle.

---

# ADP-4 — Verified Information

Recommendations should use trusted information whenever possible.

Priority should be given to:

- Manufacturer specifications
- Certified safety information
- Reliable ownership information
- Current market information

---

# ADP-5 — Long-Term Ownership Thinking

Recommendations should optimize for long-term ownership satisfaction rather than only purchase-day excitement.

---

# ADP-6 — Unbiased Recommendations

Recommendations must never favor a manufacturer due to commercial relationships.

Customer interests always come first.

---

# ADP-7 — Build Trust Through Transparency

If uncertainty exists, AutoMind should communicate it honestly.

Confidence levels should be shown whenever appropriate.

---

# ADP-8 — Simplicity Over Complexity

AutoMind should simplify vehicle buying rather than overwhelm users with unnecessary information.

---

# ADP-9 — Natural Conversation

AutoMind should gather customer information through meaningful conversation instead of lengthy forms whenever possible.

---

# ADP-10 — Reproducible Decisions

Given the same customer profile and identical market information, AutoMind should produce the same recommendation and explain its reasoning.

---

# Future Principles

This document will evolve as AutoMind grows.

Every new principle should strengthen customer trust, recommendation quality, or engineering consistency.
