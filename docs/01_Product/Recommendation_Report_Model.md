# Purpose

The Recommendation Report is the final output produced by AutoMind AI.

Its purpose is not simply to recommend a vehicle but to help customers confidently make one of the largest financial decisions of their lives.

Every recommendation must be transparent, personalized, and explainable.

---

# Report Sections

## 1. Recommendation Summary

Displays:

- Recommended Vehicle
- Variant
- Overall Match Score
- Recommendation Confidence

---

## 2. Why This Vehicle?

Explain why this vehicle fits the customer.

Example:

Excellent family comfort.

Strong long-term reliability.

Fits your annual driving.

Good resale value.

Within comfortable budget.

---

## 3. Customer Summary

Show AutoMind's understanding.

Example

Family of four

Budget ₹20 lakh

18,000 km/year

Automatic SUV

10-year ownership

High safety priority

---

## 4. Strengths

Show major advantages.

Example

Excellent reliability.

Comfortable highway ride.

Low maintenance.

Strong resale.

---

## 5. Trade-offs

Every recommendation should include disadvantages.

Example

Fuel economy slightly lower.

Technology features fewer than competitors.

Waiting period longer.

---

## 6. Ownership Outlook

Estimate

Fuel

Insurance

Maintenance

Depreciation

Resale

Overall Ownership Cost

---

## 7. Market Intelligence

Current Market Situation

Examples

Discount available.

Facelift expected.

Recall announced.

High waiting period.

Best buying month.

---

## 8. Alternative Recommendations

Top 3 alternatives.

Explain why they ranked lower.

---

## 9. Recommendation Confidence

Explain confidence.

Example

92%

Reason

Complete customer profile.

Verified market data.

Reliable ownership information.

---

## 10. Final Advice

Summarize recommendation.

Examples

Buy Now

Wait

Test Drive Before Buying

Consider Used

Increase Budget

Reduce Budget
