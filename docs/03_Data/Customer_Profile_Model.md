# Customer Profile Model

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Author: Angappamoorthy A

Created: 02-Aug-2026

Project: AutoMind AI

---

# Purpose

The Customer Profile Model defines all information AutoMind AI should understand about a customer before generating vehicle recommendations.

This profile becomes the foundation for every recommendation made by the platform.

---

# Design Philosophy

AutoMind does not recommend vehicles based only on budget.

It first understands the customer.

The better AutoMind understands the customer, the better the recommendation.

---

# Customer Profile Categories

AutoMind organizes customer information into the following categories.

1. Personal Profile
2. Family Profile
3. Financial Profile
4. Driving Profile
5. Ownership Profile
6. Vehicle Preferences
7. Emotional Preferences
8. Decision Priorities

---

# 1. Personal Profile

Purpose

Understand who the customer is.

Information

- Age (Optional)
- City
- State
- Country
- Occupation (Optional)

---

# 2. Family Profile

Purpose

Understand how the vehicle will be used by family members.

Information

- Family size
- Children
- Elderly parents
- Pets
- Primary passengers

---

# 3. Financial Profile

Purpose

Understand financial comfort rather than only purchase budget.

Information

- Budget
- Purchase method
- EMI preference
- Ownership duration
- Annual running estimate

---

# 4. Driving Profile

Purpose

Understand how the vehicle will actually be used.

Information

- City driving percentage
- Highway driving percentage
- Daily commute
- Long-distance travel
- Road conditions
- Parking constraints

---

# 5. Ownership Profile

Purpose

Understand long-term expectations.

Information

- Planned ownership years
- Service expectations
- Maintenance tolerance
- Resale importance
- Reliability importance

---

# 6. Vehicle Preferences

Purpose

Understand explicit customer choices.

Information

- Body type
- Fuel type
- Transmission
- Seating capacity
- Brand preferences
- Brand exclusions

---

# 7. Emotional Preferences

Purpose

Capture factors that influence buying decisions beyond specifications.

Information

- Driving enjoyment
- Premium feel
- Technology preference
- Design preference
- Dream car aspirations

---

# 8. Decision Priorities

Purpose

Understand what matters most to the customer.

Example priorities include:

- Safety
- Reliability
- Fuel Economy
- Performance
- Comfort
- Practicality
- Technology
- Resale Value
- Low Maintenance
- Brand Value

---

# AutoMind Principle

Customer information should be collected naturally through conversation whenever possible.

Users should never feel they are completing a lengthy questionnaire.

---

# Profile Completeness

AutoMind should estimate how complete the customer profile is before generating recommendations.

Example

40% Complete

↓

Needs additional information

85% Complete

↓

Recommendations possible

98% Complete

↓

High confidence recommendation

---

# Future Enhancements

Future versions may include:

- Climate considerations
- Charging availability
- Garage size
- Accessibility requirements
- Lifestyle prediction
