# Purpose

The Vehicle Knowledge Model defines all information AutoMind AI should understand about every vehicle before evaluating its suitability for a customer.

Rather than storing only specifications, AutoMind builds a comprehensive understanding of every vehicle from multiple perspectives including ownership, safety, technology, comfort, reliability, and long-term value.

---

# Design Philosophy

A vehicle should never be represented only by specifications.

Instead, every vehicle should be represented by how it performs throughout its ownership lifecycle.

---

# Vehicle Knowledge Categories

AutoMind organizes vehicle information into the following categories.

1. Basic Information
2. Powertrain
3. Performance
4. Safety
5. Comfort
6. Practicality
7. Technology
8. Ownership
9. Reliability
10. Market Intelligence
11. Community Intelligence

# Knowledge Sources

AutoMind should collect information from trusted sources.

Priority

1. Manufacturer
2. Certified Safety Organizations
3. Professional Automotive Reviews
4. Verified Owner Experiences
5. Automotive News
6. Community Discussions

- Static
- Periodically Updated
