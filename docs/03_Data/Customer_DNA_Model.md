# Purpose

The Customer DNA Model represents a customer's automotive personality rather than simply storing demographic information.

It converts customer facts, preferences, lifestyle, and conversation into a structured set of decision characteristics used by AutoMind's Decision Engine.

---

# Design Philosophy

Customers rarely understand or explicitly state all of their priorities.

AutoMind should infer decision characteristics from natural conversations, driving habits, ownership goals, and lifestyle.

The Customer DNA becomes the customer's decision fingerprint.

---

# DNA Dimensions

## Family Focus

Measures how strongly family requirements influence vehicle selection.

---

## Safety Focus

Represents the importance placed on occupant safety.

---

## Reliability Focus

Represents preference for dependable long-term ownership.

---

## Budget Sensitivity

Represents willingness to optimize purchase and ownership costs.

---

## Ownership Cost Focus

Measures concern for fuel, maintenance, insurance, and depreciation.

---

## Comfort Priority

Represents importance of ride quality and passenger comfort.

---

## Driving Enthusiasm

Represents preference for driving enjoyment and performance.

---

## Technology Preference

Represents interest in modern vehicle technology.

---

## Luxury Preference

Represents desire for premium materials, features, and brand image.

---

## Practicality Focus

Represents preference for everyday usability.

---

## Adventure Lifestyle

Represents interest in touring, rough roads, and outdoor activities.

---

## Sustainability Preference

Represents preference for environmentally friendly mobility.

---

# DNA Generation

Customer DNA is generated by combining:

- Customer Profile
- Conversation Analysis
- Behavioural Inference
- Explicit Preferences
- Ownership Goals

---

# Confidence

Each DNA dimension includes a confidence level.

Example

Reliability Focus

Value: 95

Confidence: 92%

Technology Preference

Value: 70

Confidence: 68%

---

# Dynamic Updates

Customer DNA evolves during the consultation.

Each new answer may strengthen, weaken, or clarify one or more DNA dimensions.
