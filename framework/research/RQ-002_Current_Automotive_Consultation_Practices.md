# 1. Research Question

How are automotive consultations currently conducted by manufacturers, dealerships, and vehicle recommendation platforms, and what lessons can be applied to the Automotive Consultation Framework (ACF)?

---

# 2. Objective

The objective of this research is to understand how the automotive industry currently assists customers in selecting vehicles and identify practices that may influence the design of ACF.

---

# 3. Scope

This research includes:

- OEM buying journeys
- Dealership consultation practices
- Online vehicle comparison platforms
- Digital automotive buying experience

This research does not include:

- Vehicle engineering
- Manufacturing processes
- Vehicle ownership lifecycle
- AI recommendation methodology

# 6. Common Evidence

The reviewed sources consistently indicate:

- Customers use multiple information sources before purchasing a vehicle.
- OEMs primarily provide tools to evaluate their own products.
- Dealerships play a broader role than vehicle sales by supporting financing, registration and after-sales services.
- Vehicle purchasing is becoming increasingly digital.
- Customer consultation quality varies across dealerships and sales consultants.
- No publicly recognized, vendor-neutral consultation methodology was identified during this research.

---

# 7. Research Summary

The automotive purchasing journey involves multiple participants including manufacturers, dealerships, digital platforms and independent information sources.

Manufacturers provide structured information about their own products, while dealerships assist customers through purchasing, financing and ownership-related services. Digital platforms further expand customer access to specifications, reviews and comparisons.

Although customers have access to extensive information, the consultation experience itself varies significantly depending on the organization and individual consultant. No publicly recognized vendor-neutral methodology describing how an automotive consultation should be conducted was identified during this research.

---

# 8. ACF Decisions

Based on the reviewed evidence, the following framework decision is adopted.

---

## AD-004

The Automotive Consultation Framework (ACF) shall define a consistent, vendor-neutral methodology for conducting automotive consultations independent of manufacturer, dealership or implementation platform.

---

# 9. Limitations

This research reviewed publicly available consultation practices and industry information.

Internal dealership training methodologies, proprietary consultation processes and unpublished organizational practices were outside the scope of this research.

---

# 10. Conclusion

Current automotive consultation practices provide customers with extensive product information and purchasing support through manufacturers, dealerships and digital platforms.

However, consultation methodologies vary across organizations, and no publicly recognized vendor-neutral framework describing a standardized automotive consultation process was identified within the scope of this research.

These findings support the need for a structured consultation framework that can be consistently applied regardless of brand, dealership or software implementation.
