# Research Domain 1

## Consumer Decision-Making Models

### Summary

Multiple widely accepted consumer behavior models describe purchasing as a sequence that generally includes:

- Need recognition
- Information search
- Evaluation of alternatives
- Purchase
- Post-purchase evaluation

These models explain consumer behavior and provide useful background for understanding purchasing decisions.

### Relevance to ACF

The reviewed models provide valuable insight into how consumers approach purchasing decisions.

However, they do not prescribe a structured methodology for conducting an automotive consultation.

ACF will use these models as foundational knowledge while defining its own consultation methodology.
