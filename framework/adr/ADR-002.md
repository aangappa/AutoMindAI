# ADR-002

## Title

Research Before Framework Design

## Status

Accepted

## Context

A framework should not be based solely on assumptions or personal experience. Its principles must be supported by existing knowledge, evidence, and validation.

## Decision

The Automotive Consultation Framework (ACF) will be preceded by a structured research phase including literature review, related work analysis, gap analysis, and problem validation.

## Consequences

- Research findings will guide framework design.
- Claims made within ACF must be supported by evidence where possible.
- The framework remains open to revision based on new evidence.
